技术与行业社团

UTAS Motorsport

动手实践技术

A student-driven Formula SAE team that designs, funds, builds and races its own open-wheel race car every year. Real project experience with industry sponsors — and members come from engineering, ICT, business and media.

时间：
Year-round; recruitment at the start of each semester (follow UTAS Motorsport on Facebook)
地点：
School of Engineering, Sandy Bay campus
谁可以加入：
All students — curious beginners welcome

志愿服务与回馈社区

Glenorchy Community Care

志愿者平价食品

Help with community events, food distribution, or offer your IT skills to a local non-profit. Run by the Churches of Glenorchy, it provides affordable groceries for low-income households (one trolley of food for $30), and volunteers can gain certification.

在 Google 地图中打开 ↗
时间：
Food access: Thursdays 9:30 – 11:00 AM; volunteering is flexible (check website)
地点：
Glenorchy Uniting Church Hall, 21 Kensington Street, Glenorchy (entrance at rear)
谁可以加入：
All students — volunteers can gain certification
官方链接
gccare.au
