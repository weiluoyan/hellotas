Your new chapter in Tasmania starts here.

You don't have to figure it out alone. Follow these steps to settle in, find your community, and build a life you love.

Built by and for international ICT students in Tasmania.

Start Your Journey

Finding a professional job needs Experience + References. Start building both from your first semester, not after graduation.

Take it one step at a time

Your essential roadmap for the first few months.

  1. STEP 1

    Settle in

    Find a place to live, set up your basic needs, and secure survival income so you can focus on study.

Build good habits, early

Make sport a habit

Free weekly Parkrun, squash clubs, trail runs — Tasmania has world-class outdoor and indoor sports that help you stay healthy and meet people outside class.

See free sports activities
