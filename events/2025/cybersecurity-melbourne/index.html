AISA CyberCon 2025 Melbourne

Three-day national cybersecurity conference bringing together industry professionals, researchers and students.

Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre (MCEC), Melbourne

15–17 October 2025 · Three days

Conference CareersOne-off GPA REQUIRED High GPA students only
Outcome: Real industry exposure outside Tasmania

About this event

AISA CyberCon Melbourne 2025, organised by the Australian Information Security Association (AISA), was held from 15 to 17 October 2025 at the Melbourne Convention & Exhibition Centre (MCEC).

Under the theme Transform to Evolve, the three-day conference brought together cybersecurity professionals, industry leaders, researchers, and students to explore emerging cybersecurity threats, technological developments, digital resilience, and industry challenges.

The conference featured keynote presentations, technical discussions, interactive activities, and professional networking opportunities. Specialised areas included the Careers Village, Knowledge Hub, Locksport Village, and Incident Response Competition Village.

For University of Tasmania students, the conference provided opportunities to connect academic learning with real-world cybersecurity practices, develop practical insights, and explore career pathways beyond Tasmania.
