School of ICT Connect & Collaborate Expo 2025 (Semester 2)

The School's second showcase, with twice as many projects as the first: 20 student teams, strategic panels, a research poster session and evening lightning talks.

Studio Theatre and Stanley Burbury Theatre, University Centre, Sandy Bay Campus, Hobart

Tuesday 28 October 2025 · 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 5:30 PM – 7:30 PM

Showcase CareerNetworking FREE Open to all ICT students
1 / 3
Outcome: 20 project teams · industry panels · research speed dating

About this event

Following the first expo in Semester 1, the School of ICT hosted its Semester 2 showcase in the Studio Theatre and Stanley Burbury Theatre. Twenty Bachelor and Master's student teams demonstrated the final-semester projects they had built with industry clients to an audience of industry, students, staff and alumni. Student games, graphics and mixed reality projects were also on show.

The program included strategic panels on how ICT is addressing education, climate and productivity, a speed-date-an-ICT-researcher poster session, and an evening session of lightning talks on AI, cyber security and gaming that was open to the public.

Organiser Dr Lindsay Wells reported an incredible turnout of industry members, alumni and current and future students. He was also recognised with a School award for Outstanding Contribution to the School's Reputation for leading the expo.

What students built

Student Information ChatBot (UTAS ICT CourseMate)

An IBM watsonx Orchestrate and Watson Discovery chatbot that helps ICT students explore course outlines, enrolment and unit questions before they enrol.

Ali Raza, Chen Xi, Hoang Giang Nguyen, Roshani Ghimire, Yu Wang, Yihang Cui

PI

Paramedic Insights

A visual analytics dashboard, built with React and .NET, that helps ambulance services spot risky trends in controlled drug records.

Aryan Bhattarai, Chantelle Kapofu, Chengyang Huang, Jackie Koo, Nathan Mullins, Valin Raika

TT

TechMate TAS

A Flutter and Firebase mobile app for ACS Tasmania that connects students and tech professionals with events, jobs and a community noticeboard.

Rakin Ahmad, Prashant Subedi, Yagya Raj Bhatta, Xuanyi Ren, Najmul Udddin, Savani Ravikumar, Md Muakchear Uddin

BH

Brainy Hacks

Brain training mobile games for the Skylight Program that help children with learning difficulties build cerebellar skills through adaptive gameplay.

Sanzida Rahman Athoy, Md Eshtyak Rahman Abir, Kanglin Wang, Jack Luttrell, Donglong Wu, Chen Gao
