EdCAT 2025: Education, Careers and Technology Summit

The ACS Education, Careers and Technology Summit in Hobart, with a dedicated career stream attended by UTAS ICT students.

Wrest Point, Sandy Bay, Hobart

Thursday 10 April 2025 · 8:15 AM – 6:00 PM

Summit CareersNetworkingIndustry Open to students, educators and tech professionals
1 / 3
Outcome: Career advice from recruiters and early-career professionals

About this event

EdCAT 2025: Education, Careers and Technology Summit was held on Thursday 10 April 2025, from 8:15 AM to 6:00 PM, at Wrest Point in Sandy Bay, Hobart. Organised by the Australian Computer Society (ACS), the full-day summit brought together students, educators, technology professionals, and industry representatives to explore developments in technology, education, and career opportunities.

The summit featured multiple conference streams covering career development, technology leadership, and education, alongside an industry exhibition. Attendees had opportunities to hear from professionals, explore industry perspectives, and connect with organisations involved in Tasmania's technology sector.

A key highlight for UTAS ICT students was the dedicated career stream, which included a panel of early-career professionals associated with ACS, Hydro Tasmania, the Department of Premier and Cabinet, and the Department for Education. The panel provided insights into early career experiences and offered practical perspectives on preparing for employment in the ICT industry.

The summit also featured discussions covering topics ranging from artificial intelligence development to job-hunting strategies. These sessions helped students connect their academic learning with industry developments while gaining a better understanding of the skills and preparation involved in entering the technology workforce.

In a LinkedIn reflection, UTAS student Yu Wang highlighted the value of meeting industry professionals and fellow students, particularly appreciating the career stream for its practical advice and personal experiences. The reflection demonstrated how participation in industry events can help students develop confidence and gain a clearer understanding of their future career pathways.

For UTAS ICT students, EdCAT 2025 provided an opportunity to broaden their knowledge of the technology industry, learn from professionals at different career stages, and build connections within Tasmania's ICT community. The event also highlighted the importance of professional networking, career preparation, and continuous learning when transitioning from university study into employment.
