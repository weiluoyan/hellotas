About this event

Industry Visit 2026 featured a visit to Huon Aquaculture, a Tasmanian salmon farming company, providing University of Tasmania (UTAS) students with an opportunity to connect with industry and gain insights into a major local business.

Huon Aquaculture operates in Tasmania's aquaculture sector, where digital technologies, automation, data management, and monitoring systems play important roles in supporting modern operations.

The visit provided an opportunity for students to gain exposure to an industry environment beyond the classroom and consider how ICT knowledge and technical skills can be applied across different sectors, including aquaculture.

For UTAS ICT students, industry visits of this kind can help broaden their understanding of local employment sectors, encourage connections between academic learning and industry practice, and support exploration of potential career pathways in technology-related roles.

Further information about the visit, including the date, location, schedule, cost, and eligibility, is currently unavailable.
