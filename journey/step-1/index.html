Survival Income

Casual work

Start job hunting from day one.

  • Tailor your resume

    Make one for each job type: customer service, hospitality or kitchen. Later, look for jobs in your field.

  • Apply online, then walk in

    Apply online, then drop in the next day with the same resume. Also try cafés, supermarkets and service stations.

  • Deliver while you wait

    With your TFN, get a free ABN and sign up for DoorDash or Uber Eats. Quick cash, but it doesn't count as job experience.

Find jobs on: Seek ↗Indeed ↗Jora ↗UTAS Careers ↗
  • Check your work-hour limit on VEVO — delivery hours count too.
  • Avoid cash-in-hand jobs — often underpaid, untaxed and a risk to your visa. Know your rights ↗

Accommodation

Live

Secure your place early — good rooms near campus go fast.

  • Search online

    Browse rooms and share houses on Flatmates and Facebook Marketplace. Start before you arrive.

  • Join local groups

    Join Hobart rental and share-house groups on Facebook. Many rooms are only posted there.

  • Ask other students

    Ask UTAS students where they live. They often know of rooms coming up in their share houses.

Find rooms on: Flatmates ↗Facebook Marketplace ↗
  • Inspect before you pay — in person or by video call. Rental scams target students booking from overseas.
  • Pay your bond through MyBond, never to the owner directly. How bonds work ↗

The Daily Basics

Eat & Groceries

  • Coles / Woolworths supermarkets
  • TUSA Food Hub — free fruit, veg and bread (bring your UTAS ID)
  • Asian grocers in North Hobart

Move & Transport

  • Metro buses are free until 30 June 2027 — no card needed
  • Check bus times on the Metro Tasmania real-time app
  • Learn to drive before you arrive — buses can be slow and infrequent

Connect & Bank

  • Prepaid SIM — Telstra, Optus, Vodafone or budget brands like Amaysim
  • Open a CommBank account online, then visit the Hobart branch with your passport
  • Stay on prepaid until you have steady income

Safety First

  • Emergency: 000 (police, fire, ambulance)
  • UTAS SafeZone app — campus security
  • Non-urgent police assistance: 131 444
