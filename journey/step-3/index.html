Rotary Clubs of Tasmania

VOLUNTEERNETWORKING

Rotary is a worldwide network of community volunteers, and Tasmania's District 9830 has 42 clubs, from Hobart and Kingston to Launceston, Devonport and Burnie. Members run local service projects, fundraisers and youth programs, and welcome students who can help with websites, IT support and events while meeting local professionals. Aged 18–30? Rotaract clubs are Rotary for young adults, with service projects, leadership training and chances to volunteer overseas.

TUSA Food Hub

VOLUNTEERAFFORDABLE FOOD ACCESS

Run by the Tasmania University Student Association (TUSA), the Food Hub is a free and low-cost grocery service for UTAS students on every campus. Book a ticket on Rubric each week: the free ticket covers fruit and vegetables, bread, frozen food and the free items table, and the $5 or $8 bundles add 5 or 8 pantry items. Student volunteers run each session, scanning tickets and helping students find what they need, and the Food Hub also hosts free cooking classes and community lunches. Sign up on TUSA's volunteering page to help out.

Open in Google Maps ↗
When:
Sandy Bay: Tuesdays & Thursdays 11 AM – 2 PM · Hobart City: Wednesdays 1 – 3 PM (tickets open at 6 PM the Friday before)
Where:
Level 1, Social Sciences Building, Sandy Bay campus · Peter Underwood Centre, Hobart City (also at Inveresk, Cradle Coast and Rozelle)
Who can join:
All UTAS students: bring your student ID and a bag; one ticket per week
Official link
tusa.org.au/food-hub

Glenorchy Community Care

VOLUNTEERAFFORDABLE FOOD ACCESS

A food relief centre run by the Churches of Glenorchy. Every Thursday morning volunteers set up the church hall, sort donated food onto the tables and pack a full trolley for each household, with bread, milk, fruit and vegetables, meat and pantry staples. Low-income households from any area are welcome: bring ID and pay a $30 handling fee for a trolley of food (they don't give out vouchers or emergency food). You can also help with community events or offer your IT skills to this local non-profit, and volunteers can gain certification.

Open in Google Maps ↗
When:
Food access: Thursdays 9:30 – 11:00 AM; volunteering is flexible (check website)
Where:
Glenorchy Uniting Church Hall, 21 Kensington Street, Glenorchy (across the pedestrian crossing from Glenorchy Primary School; follow the Entrance signs)
Who can join:
Volunteers: all students (certification available) · Food: low-income households from any area
Official link
gccare.au
